Nallamilli Venkanna Babu, also known as NVB, S/o Nallamilli Appa Rao, hails from Rayadu Pakala and was born on 17-01-1987. He is a seasoned real estate professional with over 20 years of experience in the industry.

He began his entrepreneurial journey at the young age of 16, demonstrating remarkable determination and vision early in life. Over the years, he has built a strong reputation for trust, reliability, and professionalism, successfully guiding clients in making valuable property investments.

Beyond his business achievements, he is also known for his generous nature, consistently dedicating more than 50% of his earnings toward charitable activities, making a meaningful impact in the community.
